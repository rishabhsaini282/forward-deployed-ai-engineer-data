# 2026 Enterprise AI Role Cheatsheet

A quick-reference guide to the modern AI engineering ecosystem, title optimization, and compensation benchmarks.

## Role Comparisons & Title Traps

### FDE vs. Solutions Engineer (The $80K Gap)
Conflating these roles costs candidates $80K–$120K annually.
*   **Solutions Engineer:** Pre-sales focus, demo creation, RFP responses. Measured by qualified pipeline/ARR. Paid $180K–$220K OTE (with 20-30% sales commission).
*   **Forward-Deployed AI Engineer:** Post-sales focus, writing production glue code inside client stacks. Measured by Net Revenue Retention (NRR). Paid $330K–$525K TC (all-cash/engineering equity).

### AI Engineer vs. ML Engineer (The 25% Gap)
*   **ML Engineer:** Legacy role focused on algorithmic code, hardware compute utilization, model training, and hyperparameter tuning.
*   **AI Engineer:** Focuses on integration, RAG pipelines, API orchestration, and automated evaluation frameworks. Earns a 15–25% premium over ML engineers.

### Context Engineer vs. Prompt Engineer
Prompt engineering as a standalone role is dead.
*   **Context Engineer:** Builds automated software pipelines, vector DB routing, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to inject secure data. Median TC: $210K.
